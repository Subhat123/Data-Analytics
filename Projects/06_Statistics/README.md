## 🎯 Analytical Competencies

- **Descriptive Statistics:** Measures of Central Tendency (Mean, Median, Mode) and Dispersion (Variance, Standard Deviation, IQR, Skewness, Kurtosis).
- **Probability Distributions:** Normal (Gaussian), Binomial, Poisson, and Exponential distributions.
- **Inferential Statistics & Hypothesis Testing:** Null and Alternative hypotheses, $p$-value interpretation, Type I/II errors, $Z$-tests, $t$-tests (One-sample, Two-sample, Paired), Chi-Square tests, and ANOVA.
- **Correlation & Regression:** Pearson/Spearman correlation coefficients and Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) regression analysis.
