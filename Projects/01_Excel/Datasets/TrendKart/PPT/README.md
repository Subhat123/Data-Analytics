# 📽️ TrendKart – Presentation & Business Reviews

## 📌 Contents Overview

This directory is designated for slide presentations (`.pptx` / `.pdf`) communicating insights, merchandising trends, and sales recommendations from the TrendKart project to business stakeholders.

---

## 🎯 Intended Deliverables

- **Market Trend Insights:** Highlighting top-selling fashion categories and peak shopping seasons.
- **Discount & Pricing Strategy:** Analysis of discount thresholds and their direct impact on margins.
- **Executive Summaries:** Condensed executive-level takeaways and operational recommendations.
