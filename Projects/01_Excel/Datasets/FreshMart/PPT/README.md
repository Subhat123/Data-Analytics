# 📽️ FreshMart – Presentation & Stakeholder Reporting

## 📌 Contents Overview

This directory is designated for executive presentation slide decks (`.pptx` / `.pdf`) summarizing the insights, analytical outcomes, and business recommendations derived from the FreshMart analysis.

---

## 🎯 Intended Deliverables

- **Executive Summary:** Overview of supermarket sales trends and high-level findings.
- **Strategic Insights:** Recommendations on stock optimization, promotional discounting, and regional expansion.
- **Visual Storytelling:** Slide presentation of dashboard charts for leadership reviews.
