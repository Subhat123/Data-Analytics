# AeroHome Manufacturing Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

I developed this Power BI dashboard to analyse manufacturing operations at AeroHome Appliances. The main objective of this project is to understand production performance, machine downtime, product quality, operational efficiency, financial performance, and supplier activities through interactive visualizations.

This dashboard helps management monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify operational issues, and make data-driven business decisions.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor planned production against actual production.
- Analyse production achievement and production shortfalls.
- Identify machine downtime and major downtime reasons.
- Track product quality, defects, and rework.
- Analyse operational efficiency and energy consumption.
- Evaluate procurement spending and supplier performance.
- Monitor material availability and supply chain risks.
- Compare performance across plants, products, machines, and shifts.

## Dashboard Pages

1. **Production & Plant Performance** – Analyse production output, planned units, production achievement, and shortfalls.
2. **Machine & Downtime Analysis** – Understand machine downtime hours, downtime events, and downtime reasons.
3. **Quality Analysis** – Monitor inspected units, defective units, defect rate, and rework rate.
4. **Operational Efficiency** – Analyse production performance, downtime, and energy consumption.
5. **Finance & Cost Analysis** – Evaluate procurement spending, unit costs, potential unit margin, and estimated defect cost exposure.
6. **Procurement & Supplier Performance** – Analyse supplier performance and procurement activities.
7. **Materials & Supply Chain Risk** – Identify material availability issues and supply chain risks.
8. **Operator & Shift Performance** – Compare production performance across operators and shifts.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Produced Units
- Total Planned Units
- Production Achievement %
- Production Shortfall
- Total Downtime Hours
- Average Downtime Hours
- Total Downtime Events
- Units Inspected
- Defective Units
- Defect Rate
- Rework Rate
- Total Energy Consumed
- Total Procurement Spend
- Potential Unit Margin
- Estimated Defect Cost Exposure

3. Navigate through the dashboard pages.
4. Use the available filters and slicers to explore the data.
